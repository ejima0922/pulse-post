Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Pulse Post ("the Service"), operated by Meta RC Pulse ("we", "us"), handles when you use the Service, and how that information is used.

1. Information we handle

  • TikTok authorization data: when you sign in with TikTok and authorize the Service, we receive and store the access tokens and basic account identifiers (such as your TikTok user ID and display name) needed to act on your behalf within the permissions you granted.
  • Content you prepare: the video files, captions, and thumbnail images you provide for posting, held only as long as needed to complete the delivery of each draft to your TikTok inbox.
  • Contact information: if you email us, we keep your email address and the contents of your message in order to respond.
  • Technical records: minimal logs needed to operate the Service securely and to diagnose errors (for example, timestamps and delivery status of each upload).

2. How we use the information

We use the information above solely to provide the posting-assistance features of the Service: preparing your content, delivering drafts to your TikTok inbox, maintaining your authorization, and responding to your inquiries. We do not use your content or account data for advertising, and we do not build profiles of users for third parties.

3. No sale of personal data

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties. Information is shared only where technically required to operate the Service — with TikTok's platform, as part of the authorized posting flow — or where disclosure is required by law.

4. Storage and security

Authorization tokens and content are stored on access-controlled systems. Access is limited to what is necessary to operate the Service. Content files are deleted after delivery is complete, except where a short retention period is needed to confirm delivery or resolve an error.

5. Your choices and deletion

  • You may revoke the Service's authorization at any time from your TikTok account settings; revoking stops any further action on your behalf.
  • You may request deletion of the data we hold about you, including stored tokens and contact records, at any time by emailing us. We will delete the requested data without undue delay, except where retention is required by law.
  • You may also ask what data we hold about you, or request corrections, using the same contact.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted on this page with its update date.

7. Contact

Questions or requests regarding this Policy may be sent to: meta.rc.pulse@gmail.com
