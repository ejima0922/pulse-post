What is Pulse Post?

Pulse Post is a web-based tool for creators who post short videos to TikTok. Preparing a post — writing the caption, choosing the thumbnail, uploading the file — takes time and attention. Pulse Post brings that preparation into one simple flow.

When your video is ready, Pulse Post sends it to your own TikTok inbox as a draft. Nothing is published on your behalf: you open the TikTok app, review the draft, and publish it yourself with one tap. Your account, your final decision.

How it works

1

Connect your account

Sign in with your TikTok account and authorize Pulse Post. You can revoke the authorization at any time.

2

Prepare your video

Add your finished video, write the caption, and set the thumbnail — all in one place.

3

Review and publish

The video arrives in your TikTok inbox as a draft. Review it in the TikTok app and publish in one tap.
